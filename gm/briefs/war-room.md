3. Waivers: run `ff waivers`. Review the "backups to injured starters" section of `ff free-agents` and this week's injury news. "No claim" is fine when nothing improves the roster.
4. Trades: first re-check every open trade idea in notes/recommendations.md against this week's facts and drop or revise any whose premise changed. Propose a new trade only if it gains measurable weekly points over the player it replaces or exploits a clear value gap; "no trade" is fine. Run the recommendation preflight for each: check with `ff trade --with <manager>`, name the opening offer, the walk-away price, the full replacement lineup, required drops, and the counterparty's best reason to decline or use a free alternative. Use `ff history <manager>` for the counterparty's tendencies.
5. League intel: anything in the changes that alters a contender/rebuilder read or a manager's needs. Update notes/managers.md with dated facts.

Log material recommendations in notes/recommendations.md. Lead with a 3-line summary of the actions to take.
